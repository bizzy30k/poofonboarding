Service Fees

6.1 Marketplace Listing. Poof lists every cannabis retail store holding a valid retail store authorization in Ontario on the Poof Marketplace, showing the store’s name and location only (a “Marketplace Listing”). A Marketplace Listing is provided at no cost, requires no integration, and is not transactable. No fee is payable by the Dispensary in respect of a Marketplace Listing.

6.2 Integrated Locations. Once Poof has connected the Dispensary’s point-of-sale system at an Authorized Location, that location becomes an Integrated Location, is transactable on the Poof Marketplace, and forms part of the Poof Retail Network. Integration is performed by Poof as part of the setup and onboarding services described in Section 3 at no extra charge.

6.3 Service Fee. The Service Fee is fifty dollars (CAD $50.00) per Integrated Location per month, plus applicable taxes. The Service Fee is billed monthly in advance and covers Poof’s costs of establishing and maintaining the integration with the Dispensary’s point-of-sale system, including the routing of Orders to that system, inventory and price synchronisation, and ongoing monitoring of the connection. Poof charges no commission, revenue share, or percentage of the Dispensary’s Revenue on any Order.

6.4 Referral Credit. Where the Dispensary registers using a valid brand referral code issued by Poof, the Service Fee for the first three (3) consecutive months following the Billing Start Date is credited in full. Standard fees apply automatically from the fourth month. Only one referral code may be applied per Dispensary, and the credit is not transferable or redeemable for cash. For clarity, a referral credit is a discount on the paid Services and does not constitute Beta Testing.

6.5 Payment Method and Billing Start Date. The Dispensary shall provide a valid payment method on registration, which Poof stores with its payment processor. Poof does not receive or store payment card numbers. No amount is charged on registration. Billing commences only on the Billing Start Date, being the date the Dispensary confirms its participation with an authorized representative of Poof, which Poof shall confirm to the Dispensary in writing. All fees are stated in Canadian dollars and are exclusive of HST, GST and any other applicable taxes, which are charged in addition.

6.6 Future Services and Fee Changes. Poof may introduce additional Services, including e-commerce and white-labeling services under Section 4, at fees set out in a Schedule to this Agreement or in an amendment. Poof may amend the fees payable under this Section 6 on not less than thirty (30) days’ prior written notice.
